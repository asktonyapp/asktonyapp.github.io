Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Infinite Cascade is a free, ad-free, endless match-three puzzle game for iPhone and iPad published by Brian Butt ("we," "us," or "our"). We designed it to work without requiring an account or sharing personal information with us.

Information we collect

Infinite Cascade does not require registration, ask for your name or contact information, use advertising trackers, or intentionally collect analytics or location data. We do not operate a server that receives your gameplay information.

Information stored on your device

The app stores game preferences and statistics, including your best score and whether milestone fireworks are enabled, locally on your device. These settings support gameplay and are not sent to us. Deleting the app normally removes this local information, although copies may remain in backups managed by you or Apple.

Optional in-app purchase

Infinite Cascade may offer a one-time purchase that unlocks additional colors. Apple processes payments through the App Store using StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment card information. The app checks Apple's purchase entitlements to determine whether you own the upgrade. Apple may process purchase and transaction information under its own privacy policies.

Third-party services

The app uses Apple platform services for distribution and, when enabled, in-app purchases. We do not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in the planned release. Apple's handling of data through its services is governed by Apple's own policies.

Children's privacy

The app does not request personal information and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you have a concern, please contact us.

Data retention and security

We do not maintain user accounts or a gameplay database. Game preferences and statistics remain on your device until removed or reset, subject to device backup and restoration. Apple's purchase records are managed by Apple.

Changes

If our features or privacy practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date.
