Chroma Match Support

Chroma Match is a relaxing, endless color-matching game for iPhone and iPad. No ads, no subscriptions, and no sound.

Need help?

Email ask.tony.app@gmail.com with a description of the problem, your device model, and your iOS version. Please do not send passwords or payment details.

Purchases

If you purchased extra colors and they are missing, open the game's upgrade page and select Restore Purchases. App Store billing and refunds are handled by Apple.
