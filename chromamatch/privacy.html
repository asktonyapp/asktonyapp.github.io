Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Chroma Match is a free, ad-free match-three puzzle game for iPhone and iPad, published by Brian Butt (“we,” “us,” or “our”). We designed it to work without creating an account or sharing personal information with us.

Information we collect

Chroma Match does not require registration, request your name or contact details, use advertising trackers, or intentionally collect analytics or location information. We do not operate a server that receives your gameplay data.

Information stored on your device

The app stores game preferences and statistics, such as your best score and whether milestone fireworks are enabled, locally on your device. These settings are used to provide game features. They are not sent to us. You can remove this local information by deleting the app, subject to any device backup or restoration features provided by Apple.

Optional in-app purchase

Chroma Match may offer a one-time purchase to unlock additional colors. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Apple may process purchase and transaction information under its own privacy practices. The app checks Apple's purchase entitlement information to determine whether the additional colors are unlocked.

Third-party services

The app uses Apple's platform services needed to distribute the app and, when enabled, to process in-app purchases. Apple's handling of information through those services is governed by Apple's policies. Chroma Match does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in the planned release.

Children's privacy

Chroma Match does not ask users to provide personal information and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you have a concern, contact us using the address below.

Data security and retention

Because we do not maintain accounts or receive gameplay data, we do not maintain a database of user gameplay information. Locally stored preferences remain on your device until they are reset, removed with the app, or otherwise handled by your device and backup settings. Apple's purchase records are managed by Apple.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if Chroma Match's features or data practices change. We will post the revised policy on this page and update the effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact ask.tony.app@gmail.com.
