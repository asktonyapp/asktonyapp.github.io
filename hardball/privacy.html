Privacy Policy

HardBall Classic ("HardBall") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when using the application.

Information Collected

HardBall does not require user accounts and does not directly collect personal information from users.

Advertising

HardBall may display advertisements using Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect device and advertising-related information in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

Google Privacy Policy

HardBall offers optional in-app purchases through Apple's App Store. Payment processing is handled entirely by Apple. The developer does not receive or store payment information.

High scores and game settings may be stored locally on your device. This information remains on your device unless removed by deleting the app.

Children's Privacy

HardBall does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
